A minimal and elegant personal landing page. This is a clean single-page design for people who want a simple and beautiful way to introduce themselves online. You can customize it with your name, your title or profession, links to GitHub and Twitter, and your email / contact section. Features include a dark, minimal, and modern design, a smooth blue color transition on page load, a lightweight and easy-to-customize structure, and a perfect fit as a personal introduction page.
