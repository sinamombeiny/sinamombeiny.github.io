# Personal Landing Page

A minimal and elegant personal landing page.

This is a clean single-page design for people who want a simple and beautiful way to introduce themselves online. You can customize it with:

- Your name
- Your title or profession
- Links to GitHub and Twitter
- Your email / contact section

### Features
- Dark, minimal, and modern design
- Smooth blue color transition on page load
- Lightweight and easy to customize
- Perfect as a personal introduction page
